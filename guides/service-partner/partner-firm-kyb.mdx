# Complete partner firm KYB

Submit Know Your Business verification for your SERVICE_PARTNER organization.

## Prerequisites

- Partner org onboarding started

Required permissions: `onboarding:kyb:write`.

## Steps

<Steps>

<Step title="Get KYB state">

```bash
curl -sS "$FLUIDE_BASE_URL/api/v1/onboarding/kyb" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $FLUIDE_ACCESS_TOKEN" \
  -H "X-Fluide-Api-Key: $FLUIDE_API_KEY" \
  -H "X-Fluide-Client-Id: fluide-developer"
```

</Step>

<Step title="Upload and submit">

Upload documents via onboarding KYB routes, then `POST /api/v1/onboarding/kyb/submit`. Distinct from [client KYB](/guides/service-partner/client-kyb).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Tips

- Firm KYB must be approved before production API access.
